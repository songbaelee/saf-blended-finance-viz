Comparator fund diagrams

Four diagrams comparing SAF's structure against precedent funds, extracted from the full colleague-facing walkthrough for standalone reference.

These four diagrams place SAF within the landscape of comparable blended-finance vehicles. FASA and MSLF2 mark the two ends of a spectrum: FASA shows an all-public capital structure with private mobilization pushed downstream, while MSLF2 shows the opposite — direct private co-investment, made possible only because its underlying revenue is already mature. AgDevCo then validates SAF's own capital-stack design specifically, showing donor capital absorbing first-loss risk to crowd in DFI capital — a structure SAF mirrors. LCF closes the sequence as the closest same-asset-class comparator, offering the sharpest and most specific contrast: a carbon-agroforestry fund operating in the same space as SAF, but with a different approach to carbon value flow and generation risk.
